Module 03

Know your GDPR and HIPAA rights

Two major rules, two regions. Which one applies depends on where you are, where the organisation is, and what it does — not on a logo.

Patients’ Rights Under GDPR (European Union)

Patient’s right Simple explanation Official source
1. Right to Be Informed Know how and why medical data is used. GDPR Arts. 12–14
2. Right of Access See and receive a copy of personal medical data. GDPR Art. 15
3. Right to Rectification Correct inaccurate or incomplete medical data. GDPR Art. 16
4. Right to Erasure Request deletion of personal data in certain cases. GDPR Art. 17
5. Right to Restrict Processing Limit how personal medical data is used in certain cases. GDPR Art. 18
6. Right to Data Portability Receive and transfer personal data electronically when applicable. GDPR Art. 20
7. Right to Object Object to certain uses of medical data. GDPR Art. 21
8. Rights Regarding Automated Decisions Receive protection against certain significant automated decisions. GDPR Art. 22
9. Right to Withdraw Consent Withdraw consent at any time when processing is based on consent. GDPR Art. 7(3)
10. Right to Complain Report violations to a data protection authority. GDPR Art. 77

Additional protection: Medical information receives special protection under GDPR Article 9, and organisations must apply appropriate security measures under Article 32.

Patients’ Rights Under HIPAA (United States)

HIPAA stands for the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act. It protects patients' health information held by organisations covered by the law.

Patient’s right Simple explanation Official source
1. Right of Access See and receive copies of medical records. 45 CFR §164.524 – HHS
2. Right to Request Corrections Request corrections to incorrect or incomplete medical records. 45 CFR §164.526 – HHS
3. Right to Privacy Information Know how medical information may be used and shared. 45 CFR §164.520 – HHS
4. Right to Request Restrictions Ask to limit certain uses or sharing of medical information. 45 CFR §164.522(a) – HHS
5. Right to Confidential Communications Ask to receive medical information privately through a preferred contact method. 45 CFR §164.522(b) – HHS
6. Right to an Accounting of Disclosures Request a record of certain disclosures of medical information. 45 CFR §164.528 – HHS
7. Right to Authorise Certain Disclosures Give or refuse permission for certain uses, such as marketing. 45 CFR §164.508 – HHS
8. Right to File a Complaint Report suspected privacy violations without retaliation. 45 CFR §164.530(d), (g) – HHS

These summaries are educational, not legal advice. Rights can have limits, especially for records kept for care. Wording follows official GDPR articles and HIPAA (HHS) citations.
